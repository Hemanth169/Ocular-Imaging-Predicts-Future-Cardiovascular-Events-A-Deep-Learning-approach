Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) are a major cause of death globally. Early detection is key for better treatment. Retinal imaging is a non-invasive, cost-effective way to predict CVDs. This study uses a deep learning model with convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and MobileNet to predict CVDs from retinal images. The model is trained on a large dataset of retinal images from both healthy individuals and CVD patients. Images are pre-processed to improve quality. The CNN model, with MobileNet as the base, learns to classify images as showing CVD or not. The model shows promising results in predicting CVDs, which can help healthcare professionals make better decisions and provide timely care. Further testing in clinical settings is needed to confirm its effectiveness.
